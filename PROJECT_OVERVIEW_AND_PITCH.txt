1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================

Every single evening in cities like Madurai, thousands of kilograms of wholesome,
freshly prepared food from restaurants, bakeries, hostel messes, and wedding
banquets are thrown into dumpsters simply because commercial kitchens close for
the night. Concurrently, thousands of individuals, shelter residents, and daily
wage earners face food insecurity.

Why has traditional charity failed to solve this?
1. FRICTION & DELAY: Donating surplus food typically requires phone calls,
   uncoordinated volunteer trips, and multiple app downloads with cumbersome
   logins. By the time someone answers, the food has spoiled.
2. SPOILAGE & BIOLOGICAL DECAY: Surplus food is perishable cargo. Cooked hot food
   has a strict bacterial safety window (often under 2.5 - 3 hours). Standard
   courier or generic delivery routing ignores food safety decay deadlines.
3. LACK OF DATA INTEGRITY & AUDIT TRAIL: In traditional charities, recorded
   "donated" portions rarely match what was actually delivered. Losses, spoilage
   en route, and phantom numbers are swept under the rug.

REPLATE solves this with a hyper-local, real-time logistics and redistribution
engine connecting food providers directly with NGO hubs, automated route planning,
and closed-loop portion lifecycle verification.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INNOVATION #1: THE DUAL-FUNNEL INTAKE ENGINE (Structured + Zero-Login QR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Most logistics systems require extensive onboarding. Replate introduces a
DUAL-FUNNEL intake system:
  a) Funnel 1 (Commercial/Predictive): Daily enterprise providers (hostels,
     bakeries) have multi-meal slot schedules (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner), POS
     billing integration auto-syncing sold items, and rolling AI predictions.
  b) Funnel 2 (Zero-App / Zero-Login Public QR Link `/donate/:ngoId`):
     A physical or digital QR poster ("Scan to donate surplus food. No app.
     No login.") placed at wedding halls, tea shops, and street markets.
     Anyone can report surplus food in UNDER 30 SECONDS with:
     - Bi-lingual English / Tamil interface.
     - Auto-detected storage conditions (Hot / Room Temp / Refrigerated)
       inferred automatically from food category so non-tech users don't fail.
     - Live geolocation detection with manual fallback.
     - Instant arrival in the NGO Inbox with real-time biological decay countdowns.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INNOVATION #2: SPATIO-TEMPORAL CLUSTERING & BIOLOGICAL DECAY-AWARE ROUTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Food is not Amazon packages. You cannot pick up hot food first and let it sit
in a van for 3 hours while picking up cookies.
Replate's Collection Planner:
  - Enforces biological safety deadlines (urgency levels: green > 2h, amber 1-2h,
    red < 1h, grey expired).
  - Respects temperature storage compatibility inside cargo vehicles.
  - Consolidates multiple pickups into optimized routes, demonstrating a 40% to
    60% REDUCTION in distance and carbon emissions compared to ad-hoc trips.
  - Handles multi-trip fleet queueing: when Van 1 is busy, subsequent trips are
    queued and automatically recalculate dynamic ETAs when the van frees up.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INNOVATION #3: 4-STAGE CLOSED-LOOP PORTION LIFECYCLE & TRANSPARENT SHORTFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No other platform maintains mathematical rigor on food portions. Replate enforces:
  Confirmed (Kitchen) >= Collected (Van) >= Received (Hub) >= Distributed (Beneficiaries)
  - Every step records an ISO timestamp (`createdAt`, `collectedAt`, `receivedAt`,
    `distributedAt`).
  - Strict non-negative quantity validation.
  - Discrepancies and shortfalls are NEVER hidden: if a provider promised 20 meals
    but the driver counted 18 loaded into the van, the system logs and displays
    "2 portions short" on driver manifests, depot intake, and NGO audit tables.
  - Provable consistency: Sum of provider impacts IDENTICALLY matches the NGO
    depot throughput numbers.
  - Automated "Data Integrity Guard" built right into the platform to audit
    invariants in real-time.

3. DETAILED MODULE BREAKDOWN & ARCHITECTURAL HIGHLIGHTS
================================================================================

A. PROVIDER PORTAL (Sri Krishna Bakes, Kaveri Mess, etc.)
---------------------------------------------------------
- Multi-Meal Slot Tabs: Tailored for Breakfast (Morning), Lunch (Afternoon),
  and Dinner (Night). Never mixes morning idlis with evening rotis.
- POS / Billing Feed Integration: Mock connectors (Petpooja, POSist) that auto-
  populate prepared quantities vs. sold quantities, estimating surplus without
  human arithmetic.
- Rolling Average Prediction: 7-day rolling historical averages calculating
  likely surplus for upcoming meal closures.
- Dynamic Handover Verification OTP: A unique 4-digit code generated per
  donation. The provider only hands food over when the driver gives the matching OTP.

B. ZERO-APP / ZERO-LOGIN PUBLIC QR PORTAL (`/donate/:ngoId`)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Instant URL accessible on any mobile browser without user authentication.
- English / Tamil language toggle (`ta` / `en`) with localized typography.
- Large touch targets (min 48px), visual category chips, and portion stepper.
- NGO Inbox triage with urgent color-coded timers (Green, Amber, Red).
- Trust Badging: "Trusted Donor" (past successful pick-ups) vs "Unverified".
- Printable & downloadable QR Code Poster generation in SVG/Canvas for NGO hubs.

C. NGO LOGISTICS HUB & ROUTE PLANNER
------------------------------------------------------------
- Confirmed Donation Pool: Aggregates real-time available donations.
- One-Click Route Optimization: Groups nearby providers into balanced trips
  under vehicle capacity limits (e.g., 65-100 meals).
- Polyline Road Routing: Visual Leaflet map rendering driver paths, turnaround
  waypoints, and depot drop-off.
- Fuel & Distance Savings Metrics: Visual comparison showing point-to-point km vs
  consolidated cluster km.

D. DRIVER TRANSIT & MANIFEST
------------------------------------------------------------
- Step-by-Step Stop Execution:
  Pending -> En route -> Arrived -> Collected (or Failed with reason).
- Driver Collection Counter: Modal allowing the driver to log the exact count
  loaded into the van. Enforces `actualQty <= confirmedPortions`.
- Automatic trip progression and queued trip auto-starts upon completion.

E. DATA INTEGRITY & AUDIT ENGINE
------------------------------------------------------------
- Atomic `ALLOCATE_TRIP` Reducer Action: Trip creation, donation status linking,
  pool removal, leftover replanning, notifications, and event logs execute in
  one atomic transaction.
- Append-Only Event Log: Tracks `Confirmed`, `Accepted`, `Scheduled`, `Collected`,
  `Received`, `Distributed`, `Failed`, and `Expired` with timestamps and actors.
- "Run Data Checks" Button: Built-in verification engine testing:
  1. No donation in two collections.
  2. No scheduled donation in the plan pool.
  3. Strict non-negative quantity lifecycle hierarchy.
  4. Sums of parts equal selector totals across both Provider and NGO views.
